QUESTIONS

Common questions

Can you build the showroom to our brand's design?

Yes. For the showroom we can construct the building to your brand's layout and specifications, and you handle the interior fit-out. We can also lease the land if you prefer to build yourself.

How long are the leases?

Showroom leases are typically 9–15 years with agreed rent increases. Restaurant and kiosk terms are flexible, from short licences to long leases.

Who handles approvals and construction?

We do. Nagarayan Developers manages land paperwork, approvals and construction, and gives you one point of contact through to handover.

Can we visit the site?

Yes. Call or WhatsApp us to fix a time, and our local team will meet you at the site on SH15.

Is there space for future expansion?

Yes. Land behind the frontage is planned for Phase 2, so a tenant who grows can discuss additional space with us early.
